# Paper collection

Exactly **40 original research papers**, with **10 PDFs per field**. Selected from **Nature**, **Nature Communications**, and **Science Advances**.

The articles were screened by title, abstract, publication type, and field relevance. Every retained article is labeled `research-article` in the full-text PMC XML and reports original experimental or computational work. Reviews, perspectives, editorials, and correction notices are excluded. The shortlist prioritizes citation influence and coverage of core research themes among openly licensed papers published in 2015–2025.

PDFs were acquired with `/homes/rzhu/Enzyme_agent/enzyme_discovery_agent/tools/fulltext.py`, using publisher URLs and the [PMC cloud dataset](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/tools/pmcaws/) where necessary. Crossref metadata, source XML identifiers, and PDF text were checked for identity; all PDFs parse successfully. The [manifest](manifest.json) records authors, DOI, abstract, research focus, article type, verification evidence, source URL, file size, and SHA-256. Retrieved 2026-10-21.

The original authors retain copyright. Each article remains under its own Creative Commons license, linked below and recorded in the manifest; some licenses restrict commercial reuse or distribution of adaptations. These licenses apply to the papers individually.
